Taking a PC apart is easy. Putting it back so it works is the skill. Three habits make it reliable:

  • One bag per part. Screws from the drive cage go in a bag marked 'cage'. Never a loose pile.
  • Photo before you pull. One phone photo of the cables before any plug comes out.
  • The POST check. After every rebuild: plug in, power on, watch for the Dell logo and the LED going solid white. No screen = stop and look again.

Example. The drive cage on the 7050 has a blue latch; it hinges up rather than lifting out. RAM clips open outward; the stick lifts straight up. Nothing in this lesson needs force.

The notch is off-centre so it fits one way only. Flip it.

The power LED stays blinking amber and nothing appears on screen. This is a…

no-boot problem (software)no-POST problem (hardware)monitor problem onlynormal start
No POST = the hardware did not pass its self-test. Go back inside.
