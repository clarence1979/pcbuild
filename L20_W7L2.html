8 minLearnteacher explains, you follow on screen

Installing a card is four moves: remove the slot cover, line the card up with the x16 slot, press straight down until the latch clicks, fit the bracket screw. Then move the monitor cable to the card. The board sees a card in x16 and uses it instead of the iGPU.

Example 1. Monitor still plugged into the board's HDMI: black screen, because the board switched output to the card. Move the cable.

Example 2. Card half-seated: the PC boots on the iGPU, the card is 'not detected'. Power off, press until the latch clicks.

Example 3. Picture appears but at low resolution and jerky: the driver is missing. Install from the maker's site or the OS driver tool.

Words

Latch
The small lever at the end of the x16 slot that locks the card.
Bracket
The metal plate on the card's edge that screws to the case.
Driver
Software that lets the OS use the card's features.
Slot cover
The blanking plate you remove to expose the slot opening.
