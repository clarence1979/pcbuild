Installing RAM has three parts: the right slots, a firm press, and a check. Dell colour-codes the clips. A matched pair goes in the two slots whose clips share a colour. Then the BIOS tells you whether it is running in dual-channel mode.

Example 1. Both new sticks in slots 1 and 2 (different colours): the PC boots, shows 16 GB, but 'Single' channel. Half the bandwidth.

Example 2. One stick only half-clicked: the PC shows 8 GB instead of 16, or does not POST at all. The fix is always reseat first.

Example 3. Keeping the old 4 GB stick plus two new 8 GB sticks: it works (20 GB total), but the odd stick may force single-channel. Simpler is better: a matched pair.
