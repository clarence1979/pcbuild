5 minLearnteacher explains, you follow on screen

Professional work ends in writing. The hand-over says what the machine is, what you did to it, how it is configured, and what is still wrong. The demo proves you can teach a skill, which is the best test of understanding it.

Example hand-over, abridged. Bench 4 · Dell OptiPlex 7050 SFF board in open frame · i5-7500, 16 GB DDR4-2400 (2×8, dual), 500 GB NVMe (OS), 240 GB SATA (data), GTX 1650 LP, 550 W ATX via 24→6 adapter, Dell button on original lead · Linux Mint 22, hostname PC-04, user student/tech, static 192.168.10.14/24, no gateway, firewall rule TCP 2323 from lab subnet · Known issue: case fan header 2 dead, fan on PSU adapter · Temps: 38 idle / 71 load.

Words

Hand-over document
What a customer or next technician needs: what was done, what is in it, how it is set up, what to watch.
Known issue
A problem you know about but did not fix, written down honestly.
Demo
Showing a skill clearly enough that someone new could copy it.
