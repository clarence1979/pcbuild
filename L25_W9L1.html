By the end of this lesson you can

  1. Remove every component from the Dell case in the planned order.
  2. Keep every screw bagged and every part protected.
  3. Finish at the checkpoint: bare board out, parts on the mat.
Driver: hands on the hardwareNavigator: reads each step aloud, ticks it off, checks before power-onSwap roles every lesson
Before you touch anything insideWrist strap on and clipped to bare metal on the case. PC unplugged from the wall. Hold boards by the edges.
Lift the board by its edges onlyNever by the cooler, the slots or any cable. One hand each side, straight up.

Follow your sheet. The order below is the safe default if your sheet differs.

Keep the CPU and cooler ON the board. The board travels with them. Fewer moves on the socket = fewer risks.
