By the end of this lesson you can

  1. Fit RAM, M.2 SSD, SATA drive, GPU and case fan on the frame.
  2. Complete the pre-power checklist and perform the first power-on.
  3. Confirm POST, BIOS detection of every part, and boot.
Driver: hands on the hardwareNavigator: reads each step aloud, ticks it off, checks before power-onSwap roles every lesson
Before you touch anything insideWrist strap on and clipped to bare metal on the case. PC unplugged from the wall. Hold boards by the edges.
Power-on ruleNobody plugs in or presses the power button until the Navigator has checked every connector and the teacher has said 'go'.
Never open a power supplyThe capacitors inside can hold a dangerous charge for days after it is unplugged. Nothing in this course needs the PSU lid off.
