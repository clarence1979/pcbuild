12 minLearnteacher explains, you follow on screen

A closed case is a wind tunnel. Front and bottom fans pull in cool air; rear and top fans push warm air out. The CPU and GPU coolers sit in that stream. Block it (dust, cables, a case against a wall) and temperatures climb.

Example 1. A PC pushed into a cupboard with the back against the wood: no exhaust path, CPU 95 °C. Pull it out 10 cm: 70 °C.

Example 2. Intake fans without filters: in a year the heatsink fins are a felt blanket. Filters on intakes, positive pressure, clean every term.

Example 3. Our open frame has no sides at all. Room air reaches everything, so a single CPU cooler is enough; the trade-off is dust, noise, and that nothing protects the board. That is why we use it on a bench, never on the floor.

Words

Intake / exhaust
Fans that pull cool air in / push warm air out.
Positive pressure
Slightly more intake than exhaust so dust cannot creep in through gaps.
Standoff
A brass post that holds the board above the metal tray.
Open frame
A case with no sides: the board sits on rails in the open air.
I/O shield
The thin plate that fits around the rear ports in a closed case.
