Technicians across the world use the same six steps. They stop you guessing.

Case 1. 'It turns on but no picture.' Identify: fans spin, LED white, monitor says no signal. Theory: monitor on the wrong port. Test: move the cable to the GPU. Picture. Fix: label the port. Document: 'cable on iGPU port after GPU install'.

Case 2. 'Random restarts when gaming.' Theory 1: PSU overloaded. Test: PSU tester under load: fine. Theory 2: overheating. Test: temperature log shows 99 °C. Fix: re-paste. Verify: 72 °C, no restarts for a week.

Case 3. 'Dead. Nothing at all.' Half-split: unplug everything but board, CPU, one RAM stick. Still dead → PSU or board. PSU BIST passes → board. Swap a known-good board → alive. Root cause: board.

Words

Theory
Your best guess at the cause, written before you touch anything.
Half-split
Remove half the parts to see which half holds the fault.
Known good
A part you are certain works, used to swap-test.
Root cause
The real reason, not the symptom.
