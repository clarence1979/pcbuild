Heat moves: CPU → paste → heatsink → air. The fan replaces warm air with cool. The board watches the temperature and spins the fan faster as it rises. If the cooler is loose or the paste is missing, the first link breaks and the CPU hits 100 °C and throttles.

Numbers to expect on a 7050: idle 30–45 °C; full load 60–80 °C with a healthy cooler. Over 90 °C under load means something is wrong.

Example. Two identical PCs: one re-pasted properly, one with the cooler screws left loose. Under load the first sits at 70 °C; the second races to 98 °C and the clock drops from 3.6 GHz to 2.0 GHz. Same chip, half the speed.

Words

Idle
Doing nothing much: desktop open, no programs working.
Load
Working hard: all cores busy.
Throttling
The CPU slowing itself down to stay below its safe temperature (about 100 °C).
PWM fan
A fan whose speed the board controls with a 4-pin plug.
