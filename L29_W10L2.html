Dell took the beeps away and put the diagnosis in the power button. It blinks amber in two groups: count the first group, pause, count the second. Look it up. Since 2012 the only beep left is 1-3-2, and it means memory.

Before the codes, sort the symptom into one of three rungs:

  1. No power: no fans, no LED → wall, lead, PSU switch, PSU BIST, front-panel plug.
  2. No POST: fans and LED but no screen → read the blink code; RAM, GPU, CPU power, board.
  3. No boot: Dell logo appears, then an error or black screen → boot order, drive cable, OS.

Example 1. 2,3 after a RAM upgrade: reseat the sticks, try one at a time.

Example 2. 3,2 after a GPU install: video. Reseat the card, check its power plug, try the iGPU.

Example 3. 2,2: PSU or cabling. Dell BIST, then the 6-pin and 4-pin.

Words

No power
Nothing happens: no fans, no LED.
No POST
Power arrives but the self-test never finishes: fans spin, no screen.
No boot
POST passes, then the OS fails to load.
Pattern 2,3
Two amber blinks, pause, three blinks: memory/CPU/board.
