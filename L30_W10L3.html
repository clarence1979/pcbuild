By the end of this lesson you can

  1. Diagnose and fix three planted hardware faults using the six steps.
  2. Log each fault with theory, test, result and fix.
  3. Hand a working PC to the next bench.
Driver: hands on the hardwareNavigator: reads each step aloud, ticks it off, checks before power-onSwap roles every lesson
Before you touch anything insideWrist strap on and clipped to bare metal on the case. PC unplugged from the wall. Hold boards by the edges.
Power-on ruleNobody plugs in or presses the power button until the Navigator has checked every connector and the teacher has said 'go'.

3 minStartwhole class

  • Benches rotate: you sit at a PC you did not build. Strap on. Log sheet ready.
  • Teacher: 'Each PC has one fault. Three rounds. Log before you touch.'

4 minLearnteacher explains, you follow on screen

Possible plants (you will not be told which): RAM half-seated · SATA data unplugged · monitor on the iGPU port · GPU not latched · front-panel plug off · 4-pin CPU power off · boot order set to USB with no USB · coin cell out (clock reset) · fan unplugged (fan warning at POST).

Use the ladder: no power / no POST / no boot. Then the code. Then one change at a time.
