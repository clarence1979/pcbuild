8 minLearnteacher explains, you follow on screen

Professional builds run from a sheet, not from memory. Lesson 25 strips the Dell. Lesson 26 mounts board, PSU, power and button on the frame. Lesson 27 fits RAM, SSD, GPU and fan, then first power-on. Each lesson ends at a checkpoint so a late bench is not a disaster.

Example 1 — dependencies. Standoffs before board. Board before CPU cooler check. PSU mounted before the 24-pin is routed. Front-panel plug before the first power-on test, or there is no button to press.

Example 2 — labels. 'MAIN', 'CPU', 'SATA-SSD', 'GPU', 'FAN', 'BTN'. A labelled lead goes on in seconds; an unlabelled one gets debated.

Example 3 — photos. Overall inside shot, then one close-up per plug. If something does not boot in Lesson 27, you compare against the photos first.

Words

Run-sheet
A numbered list of what happens, in order, with who does it.
Before-photo
Pictures of every connection before you unplug anything.
Dependency
A step that cannot happen until another is done (e.g. standoffs before board).
Checkpoint
A moment where the Navigator checks before you go on.
