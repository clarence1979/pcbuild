ReBuild Lab: PC Building, Repair & Maintenance

Year 9/10 elective · 18 weeks · 54 lessons of 45 minutes · 20 students in pairs on ten Dell OptiPlex 7050 SFF benches.

Each lesson page runs the 45 minutes on a timer, carries its own animated figures, and holds a printable A4 worksheet (the button in the right column).

Weeks 1–6 · Know the machineEvery part of the Dell 7050: what it does, how to remove and refit it, how to test it.
Weeks 7–11 · Upgrade and transplantGPU, PSU, open frame, the three-lesson rebuild, then troubleshooting and a fresh OS.
Weeks 12–18 · Connect and playCables, IP, the lab LAN, a Python chat server, a LAN party, then the capstone.

Week 1Safety and anatomy

Lesson 1 · Welcome to the bench: what we will build, and how we stay safeExplain what the course builds over 18 weeks (upgrade, transplant, LAN).Lesson 2 · Anatomy tour: every part inside the Dell and what it doesPoint to and name 12 parts inside the 7050.Lesson 3 · Teardown level 1: out, in, and it still bootsRemove and refit the drive cage and a RAM module safely.

Week 2The processor and its cooler

Lesson 1 · How a CPU works: fetch, decode, executeDescribe the fetch-decode-execute cycle.Lesson 2 · Sockets and coolers: taking the heat off the processorIdentify the LGA1151 socket, its lever and the alignment triangle.Lesson 3 · Thermals lab: re-paste, refit, measure the differenceApply thermal paste and refit the cooler in the X pattern.

Week 3Memory

Lesson 1 · Memory: what RAM is, what the numbers meanExplain why RAM is fast but forgets.Lesson 2 · Install RAM: pairs, slots and the BIOS checkInstall two DIMMs in the correct paired slots.Lesson 3 · Memory faults: testing RAM and reading the signsRun a memory test and read its result.

Week 4Storage

Lesson 1 · Storage: hard drives, SATA SSDs and NVMeCompare HDD, SATA SSD and NVMe on speed, moving parts and price.Lesson 2 · Install an NVMe SSD and cable a SATA driveFit an M.2 NVMe SSD into the 2280 slot with the retaining screw.Lesson 3 · Disks in the operating system: partition, format, benchmarkInitialise a new disk with GPT and create a formatted partition.

Week 5Motherboard and firmware

Lesson 1 · The motherboard map: chipset, slots, headers and form factorsTrace how the CPU, chipset and slots connect.Lesson 2 · Firmware: BIOS/UEFI, POST and the boot orderExplain what firmware does before the OS starts.Lesson 3 · Front panel and the Dell problem: buttons, LEDs and adaptersRead a standard front-panel header pinout.

Week 6Power

Lesson 1 · Power supplies: volts, watts, rails and connectorsExplain what a PSU does and name its three main rails.Lesson 2 · Power budget: size the PSU, then test itAdd up a build's power draw and choose a PSU with headroom.Lesson 3 · Cabling the ATX PSU: dry run with the 24→6 adapterRoute and connect every PSU cable on the bench without power.

Week 7Graphics

Lesson 1 · Graphics cards: what a GPU does and how it fitsExplain why games need a GPU and spreadsheets do not.Lesson 2 · Install the graphics card and get a pictureSeat a card in the PCIe x16 slot with the latch and bracket screw.Lesson 3 · Benchmark: iGPU vs card, and what the numbers meanRun a graphics benchmark and read FPS and temperature.

Week 8Cases and the transplant plan

Lesson 1 · Cases and cooling: airflow, standoffs and the open frameDescribe the path air takes through a case and why.Lesson 2 · The compatibility audit: will the Dell board fit, and what else do we need?Measure the Dell board's mounting holes and compare with the frame.Lesson 3 · Transplant run-sheet: order of operations, labels and the before-photoWrite a step-by-step run-sheet for next week's three lessons.

Week 9The transplant

Lesson 1 · Rebuild 1 of 3: strip the DellRemove every component from the Dell case in the planned order.Lesson 2 · Rebuild 2 of 3: mount the board, PSU, power and button on the frameFit standoffs in the matched holes and mount the board with washers.Lesson 3 · Rebuild 3 of 3: RAM, SSD, GPU, fan, cable management and first power-onFit RAM, M.2 SSD, SATA drive, GPU and case fan on the frame.

Week 10Troubleshooting

Lesson 1 · Troubleshooting method: six steps and a logUse the six-step troubleshooting method on a described fault.Lesson 2 · Reading the machine: Dell LED codes, the one beep and the no-POST ladderDecode a Dell amber blink pattern using the table.Lesson 3 · Fault lab: planted faults, timed diagnosisDiagnose and fix three planted hardware faults using the six steps.

Week 11Operating system

Lesson 1 · Boot media and the install: USB, boot menu, partitionsCreate a bootable USB installer.Lesson 2 · First boot: drivers, updates, accounts and the lab imageInstall drivers and updates on a fresh OS.Lesson 3 · Software troubleshooting: boot problems, safe mode, disk health and backupsDiagnose a no-boot that is software, not hardware.

Week 12Networks: foundations

Lesson 1 · Networks: NICs, MAC addresses, switches and what a LAN isDefine LAN, NIC, MAC address and switch.Lesson 2 · How data travels: frames, packets, ports and the four layersDescribe the four-layer model and what each layer adds.Lesson 3 · Cables and standards: Cat5e/6, twisted pairs, RJ45 and the rulesExplain why Ethernet pairs are twisted and what Cat5e/Cat6 mean.

Week 13Cable making and testing

Lesson 1 · The T568B pinout: colours, order and why both ends matchRecite the T568B wire order from memory.Lesson 2 · Make a patch lead: strip, order, trim, insert, crimpTerminate one end of a Cat5e cable to T568B.Lesson 3 · Test, diagnose, fix: the LAN tester and what each light meansUse a cable tester to pass or fail a patch lead.

Week 14IP addressing

Lesson 1 · IP addresses: dotted decimal, binary and the private rangesConvert an octet between decimal and binary.Lesson 2 · Subnet masks: who is on my network, and who needs a routerExplain what a /24 subnet mask does.Lesson 3 · Static IP, link lights and the first pingSet a static IP address and mask on Windows and Linux.

Week 15Building the LAN

Lesson 1 · Build the lab LAN: cable every bench, ping every neighbourConnect 20 PCs to the switch with a documented port plan.Lesson 2 · Network troubleshooting: the bottom-up ladder on planted faultsApply the bottom-up ladder to find a network fault in under five minutes.Lesson 3 · Ports, sockets and telnet: talking to a program by its door numberExplain what a port and a socket are.

Week 16Sockets, chat and firewalls

Lesson 1 · Write a chat server in Python: sockets, bind, listen, acceptExplain what each socket call does: socket, bind, listen, accept, recv, send.Lesson 2 · Write the chat client, run a class-wide chat, and read the trafficWrite a socket client that sends and receives at the same time.Lesson 3 · Firewalls and services: open exactly one doorExplain what a host firewall does and why the default is 'block inbound'.

Week 17LAN party and the wider network

Lesson 1 · LAN party setup: host a game server and connect every benchHost a multiplayer game server on one bench and join from the others over the LAN.Lesson 2 · LAN party: play, measure, and see the network under loadPlay a 20-player LAN session and keep it running.Lesson 3 · From LAN to WAN: routers, DHCP, DNS and why the lab is an islandExplain what a router, DHCP and DNS add to a network.

Week 18Capstone and wrap-up

Lesson 1 · Capstone 1: full fault diagnosis under timeDiagnose and repair two unknown faults (one hardware, one network) on a PC you did not build.Lesson 2 · Capstone 2: the technician's hand-over document and a 3-minute demoWrite a one-page hand-over for your rebuilt PC: parts, settings, IP, known issues.Lesson 3 · Wrap-up: showcase, decommission the lab, what comes nextPresent a showcase demo to the class.
