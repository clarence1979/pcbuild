4 minLearnteacher explains, you follow on screen

The board goes on standoffs only where the audit said MATCH. Every screw gets a nylon washer. Then the PSU is mounted so the 24-pin reaches the adapter without crossing the CPU cooler. Then the button, so Lesson 27 can test.

Keep the board's rear USB housing clear of any metal rail. That is the one that kills boards.

Words

Matched hole
A board hole with a frame hole/rail under it, from the audit.
Washer
A nylon ring between the screw head and the board.
Snug
Tight enough that nothing moves; not so tight the board bends.
