A benchmark draws the same scene on every PC so numbers can be compared. FPS above 60 looks smooth; below 30 feels choppy. Watching the GPU temperature and load at the same time tells you whether the card is working hard (99% load) or waiting on the CPU (GPU load low, FPS low = CPU bottleneck).

Example 1. HD 630 iGPU at 1080p: 8 FPS. GTX 1650: 60 FPS. Same scene, 7× faster.

Example 2. Drop resolution to 720p: the iGPU doubles to 16 FPS. Fewer pixels per frame = less work.

Example 3. A fast card with an old CPU: GPU load sits at 50%, FPS does not rise with lower settings. The CPU cannot feed it frames fast enough.

Words

FPS
Frames per second: how many pictures the GPU finishes each second.
Resolution
Pixels across × down: 1920×1080 is 1080p.
Bottleneck
The slowest part that holds the rest back.
Thermal throttling
The GPU slowing itself down when hot.
